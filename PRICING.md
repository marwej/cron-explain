# cron-explain — Pricing

**Plan:** Single tier, "Pay-what-it's-worth" one-time license.
**Price:** $9 one-time (per seat), or free for open-source/personal use under MIT with a "buy me a coffee" prompt.

**Reasoning:**
- It's a stdlib-only CLI script, not a hosted service with ongoing infra cost — no recurring cost to us, so no subscription needed.
- Developers hit "what does this cron mean" rarely but painfully (debugging a misfiring job at 2am). Willingness to pay is low-effort/high-relief, not recurring — a one-time small fee matches that.
- $9 is below the "ask my manager" threshold for an individual dev, high enough to signal it's worth taking seriously, and priced like a small utility (comparable to a Raycast extension or a JetBrains plugin add-on).
- Free/open tier keeps it usable as a lead magnet (drives GitHub stars, word of mouth) while the paid tier is for teams who want support/updates bundled.
